Pick up a tape

The tape winds forward as you finish lines

    One line at a time, at your pace

    01

    Hear it once

    The video stops at the end of every line. Too fast? Replay it with Tab, or Shift+Tab at 0.75× speed.

    02

    Type it blind

    The line stays hidden as dots. Each correct letter shows up; a wrong one buzzes. Punctuation fills in for you, and accents never need typing.

    03

    Never stay stuck

    Miss the same letter three times and it's revealed. Ctrl+H shows a letter, twice in a row the whole word. Video and text out of step? Nudge the timing offset.
